Source resolution and recipient RLS remain the Phase 1 protected behavior. Browser, anon and service API roles cannot invoke the worker. Active membership and portal/client state rechecked under locks; staff memberships are excluded. A transaction-wide advisory lock, preference row locks, atomic insert/advance and unique recipient/client/due key prevent duplicates and make failures retryable. Batch limit 100 eligible recipients. Disabled recipients are excluded before the limit so they do not block active recipients.

Daily = one UTC day, weekly = seven UTC days, monthly = one UTC calendar month, from save/frequency change or latest delivery. New setup schedules first delivery after one selected interval. Category saves preserve due date. Overdue activity is combined into one digest and then the next date advances from delivery time, avoiding backlogs of empty/repeated messages.
